## Hook analysis

- **Hook (first 2 s):** Bold, all-caps claim with a visual. Strong.
- **Open loop:** "Killed 3D modeling" is held until the proof at 8 s.
- **Proof before explanation:** Renders appear before the process is explained.
- **Concrete example:** A specific object (headphone hook) makes the claim believable.
- **Trust signals:** Free, open-source repo; real build time; printability checks.
- **CTA:** Comment keyword ("3D") with a DM link. This is a standard lead-capture mechanic.

## Reusable template

1. Claim-style hook in bold text over a 3D render.
2. Show the free / open-source proof.
3. Show finished outputs before the method.
4. Use one concrete prompt, typed on screen.
5. Show the real build time.
6. Show the validation or quality check.
7. Show the end result (physical print).
8. Close with "No X. No Y. Just one sentence." followed by a keyword CTA.

- Only the captions and frames were analysed. The spoken script and any voiceover were not transcribed.
