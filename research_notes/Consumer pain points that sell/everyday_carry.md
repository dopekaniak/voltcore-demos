# Everyday Carry (Travel Accessories, Commute Gear, Leather Goods) as a Consumer Pain Point for Small Online Gift and Home-Goods Stores

Research date: 2026-11-04. Scope: US consumer data, 2023-2026 preferred. Older figures are labeled. Figures marked UNVERIFIED were seen only in a search snippet or an unnamed secondary source. Figures marked CONFLICT disagree with another source; both are listed.

## Market size and growth: How large is the US travel-accessory and leather-goods market, and how fast is it growing?

### Takeaway
No credible standalone US dollar figure for travel accessories was found. Third-party estimates for the US leather goods and luggage category cluster around $50-60B (2024-2025), with projected growth of about 3.7-6.5% per year. Global travel-accessory estimates for 2025 cluster around $54-60B at about 6.3-6.4% CAGR. Vendor estimates differ widely on definitions, so use them only as directional.

### Cited Findings
- US travel accessories: projected CAGR of 7.02% for 2025-2030. The snippet gives no 2025 dollar value. Single publisher. — [Knowledge Sourcing, USA travel accessories market](https://www.knowledge-sourcing.com/report/usa-travel-accessories-market)
- North America travel accessories: 2025 value US$15.402B, CAGR 6.34% through 2030. — [Knowledge Sourcing, North America](https://www.knowledge-sourcing.com/report/north-america-travel-accessories-market)
- Global travel accessories, 2025 estimates: about USD 54.46B (Expert Market Research, 6.30% CAGR 2026-2035) — [Expert Market Research](https://www.expertmarketresearch.com/reports/travel-accessories-market); US$58.551B (Knowledge Sourcing, 6.40% CAGR to 2030) — [Knowledge Sourcing, global](https://www.knowledge-sourcing.com/report/global-travel-accessories-market); USD 60.17B (GII Research, 6.4% CAGR 2025-2032) — [GII Research](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/tsci1488721-travel-accessories-market-global-industry-size.html)
- CONFLICT: Valuates projects the global market reaching USD 95.7B by 2031 at 6.4% CAGR. The base year and definition were not captured. — [PR Newswire, Valuates](https://prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/travel-accessories-market-size-to-grow-usd-95-7-billion-by-2031-at-a-cagr-of-6-4--valuates-reports-302114595.html)
- US leather goods and luggage: USD 52.2B in 2024, projected to reach USD 85.4B by 2032 (6.5% CAGR, 2025-2032). — [PS Market Research](https://www.psmarketresearch.com/market-analysis/us-leather-good-luggage-market-report)
- US leather market (broad, includes automotive interiors): USD 56.68B in 2025, projected USD 99.23B by 2034 (6.42% CAGR). — [Market Data Forecast](https://www.marketdataforecast.com/market-reports/united-states-leather-market)
- CONFLICT: Emergen Research: US leather market USD 12.5B (2024), 3.7% CAGR 2025-2034. — [Emergen Research](https://www.emergenresearch.com/industry-report/us-leather-market)
- NARROW DEFINITION: IBISWorld puts US Leather Goods & Luggage Manufacturing at $3.6B in 2025, down 0.4% in 2025, with a 2.4% CAGR for 2020-2025. This covers manufacturing only, not retail sales. — [IBISWorld](https://img3.ibisworld.com/united-states/market-size/leather-good-luggage-manufacturing/374)
- CONFLICT: Kentley Insights puts US leather goods manufacturing industry sales at $6.3B in 2024, which does not match IBISWorld's 2025 figure. — [Kentley Insights via MarketResearch.com](https://www.marketresearch.com/Kentley-Insights-v4035/Leather-Goods-Manufacturing-Research-Updated-40656179)
- EXCLUDED: Market Research Future claims a US market of about USD 131.38B in 2025. The page URL does not match its title, so this was not used.
- Growth-only data point: Newstrail reports US leather goods growth of 5.5%, with no dollar value. UNVERIFIED. — [Newstrail](https://www.newstrail.com/u-s-leather-goods-market-forecast-2025-2035-trends-and-growth-insights-2/)

### Inferences
- The most defensible US framing is roughly $50-60B for broad leather goods and luggage (retail plus manufacturing, varying by publisher), growing roughly 4-6.5% per year. Travel accessories are a subset of that, and no source isolates the US travel-accessory slice.
- Vendor estimates differ by a factor of about 10 depending on whether they measure manufacturing output, retail sales, or a broad leather category. Any report used in a deck or business plan should state its definition.

### Gaps
- No US-only, 2023-2026 dollar figure for travel accessories (bags, organizers, tech travel gear, wallets) was found. Searches returned only global or North America estimates.
- Paid reports (Statista, IBISWorld full reports, Grand View Research, Euromonitor) were not accessible. Their US travel-accessory series should be checked directly before any figure is reused.
- Firecrawl search returned HTTP 401 during this session, so research relied on WebSearch and page fetches. Search coverage is therefore narrower than planned.

## Conversion, AOV, and return rates: What is reported for travel and commute accessories online?

### Takeaway
Reported online conversion rates for luggage, bags, and travel accessories range from about 0.8% to 1.8%, below the e-commerce average. Return rates are reported at about 12.9%-15%, below the e-commerce average of about 17.5%. Average order value estimates (about $100) come from one lightly verified source. The sources conflict, and the most recent fetched page gives the lowest conversion figure.

### Cited Findings
- Wisepim luggage benchmark page (fetched; updated Oct 17, 2026): average conversion rate in luggage, bags and travel accessories e-commerce is 0.8%, per Salesforce (Q2 2026). E-commerce average is 2.68%, per Dynamic Yield (Oct 2025-Sep 2026). — [Wisepim, luggage benchmarks](https://wisepim.com/ecommerce-industry-insights/luggage-bags)
- Same page: average return rate is 12.9%, per ECDB (no date stated). E-commerce average return rate is 17.6%, per NRF and Appriss Retail (2023). — [Wisepim, luggage benchmarks](https://wisepim.com/ecommerce-industry-insights/luggage-bags)
- CONFLICT: An earlier Wisepim version (search snippet, dated May 2026) gave luggage conversion of 1.8% vs. a 2.3% e-commerce average; mobile conversion 1.5% vs. 2.85%; AOV about $100 vs. $143 for e-commerce overall; return rate 15% vs. 17.5%. The fetched page does not confirm the AOV or mobile figures. UNVERIFIED for AOV and mobile conversion. — [Wisepim, luggage benchmarks](https://wisepim.com/ecommerce-industry-insights/luggage-bags)
- Shopify (Q1 2024, from search snippet, not fetched): general handbags and luggage conversion 0.9%, vs. about 2% for general apparel. Shopify's guidance calls 2.5-3.5% ideal. Shopify's own figures are older and cover a different window. — [Shopify, CRO statistics](https://www.shopify.com/blog/cro-statistics)
- Grips Intelligence luggage category (modeled estimates from a browser-extension tool, not audited): conversion band 1.5-2.0% and AOV band $0-100. Retailer-level conversion ranges from under 0.5% to about 2.5%. Lowest observed AOV was $79. These are estimates, not reported sales. — [Grips Intelligence, luggage bags](https://gripsintelligence.com/insights/industries/luggage-bags)
- GemPages blog (Q4 2025 cross-industry table): handbags and luggage about 1.4%. Underlying source not named. UNVERIFIED. — [GemPages, Shopify conversion benchmarks](https://gempages.net/blogs/shopify/shopify-conversion-rate-benchmarks-optimization)
- Return rates for bags generally (2026 roundup, source not identified): clothing 26%, bags 19%, shoes 18%. UNVERIFIED. — [WISEPIM, return statistics](https://wisepim.com/ecommerce-return-rate-statistics)
- US consumer survey (2023): 18% of online buyers said they returned bags, accessories, or shoes bought in the prior 12 months. Source link not captured. UNVERIFIED.
- Practical Ecommerce: notes the luggage and travel-accessories market fell 60% in 2020, and credits mid-priced DTC brands with setting conversion practices. Specific rates not retrieved. — [Practical Ecommerce, luggage conversion lessons](https://www.practicalecommerce.com/conversion-lessons-from-the-luggage-industry)
- Travelbags case study (Returnless): high return costs driven by a small slice of the assortment. Qualitative only. — [Case study, Travelbags](https://www.casestudies.com/company/returnless/case-study/travelbags-customer-case-study)

### Inferences
- A working benchmark for a small store is conversion of roughly 1-2% and return rate of roughly 12-19%. The 0.8% (Wisepim, 2026) figure is the lowest current reading and may reflect a conservative sample.
- Returns in luggage are driven by size and capacity mismatch (Wisepim attributes this to dimension issues). Clear dimension specs and fit guidance are a plausible lever for reducing returns. This is an inference, not a sourced finding.
- Small-store AOV is likely below the $143 e-commerce average. The $100 figure is plausible but not confirmed.

### Gaps
- No primary-source (retailer-reported) conversion or AOV figure for travel accessories was found. All figures come from vendor benchmark pages or modeled tools.
- Return rate source for the 12.9% and the 18% survey figure is not primary. Methodology and year are unknown for both.
- Commute gear (backpacks, sling bags, tech organizers) is not separated from luggage in any source found.

## Seasonality: How does travel-accessory demand peak compared with the Nov-Dec gift window?

### Takeaway
One vendor benchmark (no named underlying data, no values) says travel demand peaks in summer (June-August) and in the winter holidays (November-December), with smaller lifts in spring break and after-travel sales. Google's holiday guide says travel accessories are typically best discounted in November, while large luggage is cleared later. No source quantifies the monthly split of travel-accessory sales.

### Cited Findings
- Wisepim (fetched): demand peaks in summer (June-August) and winter holidays (November-December). Moderate increases around spring break (March-April). Lifts from Black Friday/Cyber Monday and post-travel sales (August-September, January). No values and no source named for this section. — [Wisepim, luggage benchmarks](https://wisepim.com/ecommerce-industry-insights/luggage-bags)
- Google holiday shopping guide: travel accessories see their best deals in November, while large luggage is typically discounted deeper later, at clearance. From search snippet, not fetched. — [Google, holiday shopping guide](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/holiday-shopping-when-to-buy-guide/)
- US Travel Association (fall 2025 forecast): total US travel spending projected to grow 1.1% to $1.35T in 2025. Domestic business travel projected to grow 1.4% in 2025. — [HospitalityNet, USTA fall 2025 forecast](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4129196/us-travel-forecast-2025-modest-growth-but-decline-in-international-visitors-threatens-economy-and-jobs)
- US Travel Association (spring 2026 update, from snippet): 2025 business travel grew 1.1% to $317B. 2026 forecast: +0.7% (real) to $319B. Total US travel spending 2026 forecast to rise 1% to $1.37T. — [Business Travel Executive, USTA 2026 update](https://www.businesstravelexecutive.com/news/usta-projects-slight-growth-in-business-travel-spending-in-2026/)
- CONFLICT: Skift headline says business travel spending was forecast to hit a record in 2025. This conflicts with USTA's modest 1.1% growth figure. Not reviewed in full. — [Skift](https://skift.com/?p=761303)
- Phocuswright, US Consumer Travel Report 2025 (October 2025, from snippet): travel incidence held steady, but spending tightened as consumers weigh travel against other financial priorities. Average annual spend declined while trip counts were unchanged. — [Phocuswright, US Consumer Travel Report 2025](https://www.phocuswright.com/Travel-Research/Consumer-Trends/US-Consumer-Travel-Report-2025)
- Phocuswright, US OTA market (from search summary, not verified on the page): OTA sales reached $108.5B in 2025, with modest year-over-year growth. UNVERIFIED. — [Phocuswire, Phocuswright research](https://www.phocuswire.com/united-states-traveler-trends-spending-phocuswright-research)

### Inferences
- Travel-accessory demand has two peaks: summer and the Nov-Dec gift window. The gift window is likely the larger peak for small gift and home-goods stores, because the same products are bought as presents. This is an inference from the sourced seasonal descriptions, not a measured split.
- Flat trip incidence with lower per-trip or annual spend (Phocuswright 2025) suggests consumers may buy fewer premium accessories, or trade up less often. This is a plausible but unproven link to accessory demand.
- Business-travel recovery is slow (USTA: about 1% growth). Commute and business-travel accessories are therefore unlikely to drive a sharp demand spike in 2026-2027.

### Gaps
- No monthly or quarterly share of US travel-accessory sales was found, so the summer vs. Nov-Dec comparison cannot be quantified from these sources.
- Wisepim's seasonality chart has no values in its text.

## Gift purchases: Are travel and commute accessories commonly bought as gifts, and how much happens in the holidays?

### Takeaway
US holiday gifting is large (NRF 2025: $627.93 per person on gifts, out of $890.49 total), and clothing or accessories are the most-desired gift category. No source measures what share of travel or commute accessories are bought as gifts, or what share of those sales falls in Nov-Dec. Travel-retail data suggests gifting is a meaningful share of purchases in that channel, but it is not a US online accessory figure.

### Cited Findings
- NRF / Prosper Insights 2025 holiday survey (released Oct 16, 2025; fetched): planned average holiday spending $890.49 per person, down 1.3% from $901.99 in 2024. Gifts for family and friends: $627.93. Food or candy, decorations, and greeting cards: $262.56. Survey of 8,247 adults, Oct 1-7, 2025, margin of error ±1.1 points. — [NRF press release](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/consumers-to-spend-second-highest-amount-on-record-according-to-nrf-holiday-survey)
- NRF 2025 (fetched, separate question): 46% said they would like clothing or accessories as gifts. Travel was not mentioned in the release. — [NRF press release](https://nrf.com/media-center/press-releases/consumers-to-spend-second-highest-amount-on-record-according-to-nrf-holiday-survey)
- NRF 2024 (via eMarketer): 54% of US holiday shoppers expected to give clothing or accessories as gifts, the most popular gift category. — [eMarketer, top gift categories 2024](https://www.emarketer.com/content/top-gift-categories-of-2024-holiday-season)
- Timing (secondary summary of the NRF 2025 survey): 42% of shoppers plan to start before November. Secondary source, not checked against NRF. — [CPA Practice Advisor](https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2025/10/16/holiday-spending-predicted-at-890-per-person-for-2025-season/171029/)
- Price pressure (secondary report): 85% of holiday shoppers expect higher prices due to tariffs. UNVERIFIED against NRF primary.
- Travel-retail gifting (m1nd-set, via India Outbound): share of travel-retail shoppers buying gifts fell from 42% (2020) to 26% (2023 and H1 2024). Airport and duty-free context, not travel accessories. — [India Outbound, m1nd-set](https://indiaoutbound.info/?p=106521)
- UNVERIFIED: a secondary source says 2025 actual holiday sales rose 3.1% to $972B. Not confirmed with NRF. — [Walnut Invest, holiday spending statistics](https://walnutinvest.com/stats/holiday-spending-statistics)

### Inferences
- The Nov-Dec window carries the largest concentration of gift spending in the US. Clothing and accessories are a top category, so travel and leather accessories sold as gifts are likely to cluster there. This is an inference from category-level data.
- A "travel-ready gift" positioning (for example, a leather organizer or carry-on accessory) fits the NRF finding that accessories are a top desired gift. Positioning is an inference, not a sourced conversion result.

### Gaps
- No US data found on the share of travel or commute accessory purchases that are gifts.
- No data found on what share of annual travel-accessory sales happen in Nov-Dec.
- The 2019 and 2020 m1nd-set airport gifting data was excluded as outside the 2023-2026 window.

## Source Conflicts and Verification Notes
- Conversion rate: Wisepim lists 1.8% (search snippet, May 2026) and 0.8% (fetched page, Oct 2026) for luggage, bags and travel accessories. Shopify (Q1 2024) lists 0.9% for handbags and luggage. GemPages lists about 1.4% (unnamed source).
- Return rate: Wisepim lists 15% (snippet) and 12.9% (fetched). Bags generally are listed at 19%.
- Global market size for travel accessories: $54B-$60B for 2025 across three publishers. Valuates projects $95.7B by 2031 (base not captured).
- Business travel: USTA shows modest growth (about 1%). A Skift headline describes record 2025 business travel spending.
- Unverified: the 18% survey return figure, the 85% tariff-price figure, the 42% early-shopping figure, the 3.1% 2025 holiday sales growth figure, and the $100 AOV figure.
