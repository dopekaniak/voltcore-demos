# Search and Review Dependence of Local Service Niches (Lake Norman / Mooresville, NC)

Research date: 2026-10-23. Scope: Mooresville, Huntersville, Cornelius, Davidson, Statesville, Concord.
Method: 14 web searches (WebSearch). Firecrawl was unavailable (401). No Google Maps pages were scraped or fetched. Local review counts below come only from search snippets and third-party directories, and most are undated.

Overall caveat: No survey found breaks out search and review dependence for the specific niches requested (HVAC, plumbing, pest control, etc.). The niche ranking in the last section is an analyst estimate built from the national survey data below. It is not a measured result.

## Which niches depend most on Google Maps and reviews for new customers?

### Takeaway
No niche-level survey was found. National data shows that most consumers read reviews and that online search is a leading first step for home-service and HVAC/plumbing buyers, but referrals still matter. Niche ranking is an estimate (see Inferences).

### Cited Findings
- 97% of US consumers still lean on reviews when making local purchase decisions; 41% "always" read reviews when browsing for businesses, up from 29% the prior year (BrightLocal 2026, n=1,002 US adults, fielded Feb 2026) — [BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) (figures reported via [Pin Me To summary](https://www.pinmeto.com/news/brightlocal-local-consumer-review-survey-2026/))
- 31% will only use businesses rated 4.5 stars or higher, up from 17% (same 2026 survey, per the secondary Pin Me To summary; not verified in the full BrightLocal report) — [Pin Me To](https://www.pinmeto.com/news/brightlocal-local-consumer-review-survey-2026/)
- 74% look for reviews written in the last three months; 80% are more likely to use a business that replies to all reviews (same survey, secondary summary) — [Pin Me To](https://www.pinmeto.com/news/brightlocal-local-consumer-review-survey-2026/)
- Google's share of review use fell from 83% (2025) to 71% (2026); consumers use an average of six review sites (same survey, secondary summary) — [Pin Me To](https://www.pinmeto.com/news/brightlocal-local-consumer-review-survey-2026/). Note: the 2025 baseline also appears as 71% "use Google to read reviews" in another secondary source, so the two baselines conflict.
- Only 4% of consumers say they never read online business reviews (BrightLocal 2025, cited by secondary blogs; original not verified) — no URL verified.
- Homeowners researching a personal referral online before calling: 74% (Scorpion, 2,000 homeowners, undated in snippet) — [Scorpion](https://www.scorpion.co/home-services/insights/blog/verticals/home-services/the-state-of-home-services-marketing-what-homeow/)
- Friends and family referrals remain the No. 1 source for home services, but their relevance has dropped; the internet rose from No. 3 (2005) to No. 1 for information (ProRemodeler trend analysis, undated) — [ProRemodeler](https://www.proremodeler.com/new-homeowner)
- Silverback Strategies (surveyed Aug 2020, 1,066 US adults who bought a service in the prior 12 months): 42% of HVAC/plumbing customers started by going online; 40% started by contacting a past contractor; for buyers under 55, nearly 60% started with an internet search; older buyers leaned toward past providers — [ACHR News](https://achrnews.com/articles/144547-homeowners-under-55-search-for-hvac-providers-on-their-smartphones) (summary page; underlying study not directly read)
- Farnsworth Group (about 954 consumers, undated): word of mouth was the primary way homeowners found pros (about 3 in 5); 20% used a pro's website or app; 14% used Angi/HomeAdvisor-type sites — no URL verified.
- Software Advice (undated): 56% used online resources (review sites, search) to find their most recent provider vs 44% traditional methods — no URL verified.
- Modernize Homeowner Index (undated): 41% would depend on website recommendations and 29% on online search results when choosing a contractor — [Solar Builder summary](https://solarbuildermag.com/news/modernize-41-percent-of-homeowners-select-contractors-based-on-website-recommendations/)
- Restaurants (Pew, Dec 2011): 51% of consumers turned to the internet for restaurant/local info (38% search engines); 23% relied on word of mouth — [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2011/12/14/where-people-get-information-about-restaurants-and-other-local-businesses/markdown)
- Restaurants (BrightLocal 2013): nearly 70% used the internet to find a restaurant or cafe in the prior 12 months; over 60% read restaurant reviews (2013, older) — no URL verified (cited in a secondary summary of BrightLocal 2013 survey).
- BrightLocal (2013): 61% had recommended a local business by word of mouth; 57% searched online for a local business more than six times in the past year — no URL verified (secondary summary).
- Local search conversion figures (76% visit within 24 hours; about 28% purchase): sources conflict (one claims 78%, another 88% with different definitions). Treat as unreliable marketing statistics — [SchedulingKit](https://schedulingkit.com/statistics/google-business-profile-statistics)
- Kelsey/BizRate (2004): local commercial searches were about 25.1% of searches by online buyers — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/local-search-gets-25-of-online-buyers/273/) (very old)
- Google Local Services Ads: a study cited by Search Engine Journal says review ratings are the biggest motivator for clicking an LSA. Local Services Ads receive about 13% of all SERP clicks (study cited; no date verified) — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-local-services-ads-receive-13-of-all-serp-clicks-study/)

### Inferences
- Across national surveys, the share of people who start with online search for HVAC and plumbing is roughly 40% to 60%, with referrals still a major share. For younger buyers, search is the usual first step. These figures come from different methods and are not directly comparable.
- Star-rating thresholds (4.0 to 4.5) and recency requirements mean a business needs a steady flow of recent reviews to appear in the consideration set. This matters most for categories with many options and no strong referral network.

### Gaps
- No current survey breaks out search or review dependence by niche (HVAC, plumbing, pest control, landscaping, salons, barbers, pet care, fitness, etc.). Trade-specific data would require a paid industry report or a custom survey.
- Original Google / Think with Google local-search study was not located. Its 76% / 28% figures could not be verified.
- No BIA/Kelsey report from the last five years was found; the most recent Kelsey local-search figure located was from 2013.
- The Software Advice and Farnsworth figures lack verified URLs and dates.

## What do published surveys say about the share of local customers who search online before hiring each type of service?

### Takeaway
National surveys show online search is now a leading first step for many home-service buyers (around 40% to 60% in the one survey that splits by channel), but referrals remain strong. Results vary by sample, year, and question wording, and no source gives trade-by-trade shares.

### Cited Findings
- HVAC/plumbing: 42% started online, 40% started with a past contractor (Silverback, 2020) — [ACHR News](https://achrnews.com/articles/144547-homeowners-under-55-search-for-hvac-providers-on-their-smartphones)
- Buyers under 55: nearly 60% started with an internet search (Silverback, 2020) — [ACHR News](https://achrnews.com/articles/144547-homeowners-under-55-search-for-hvac-providers-on-their-smartphones)
- Homeowners: 74% research a referral online before calling (Scorpion, undated) — [Scorpion](https://www.scorpion.co/home-services/insights/blog/verticals/home-services/the-state-of-home-services-marketing-what-homeow/)
- Restaurants: about 38% used search engines, 23% word of mouth (Pew 2011) — [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2011/12/14/where-people-get-information-about-restaurants-and-other-local-businesses/markdown)
- Trade-specific survey data for pest control, landscaping, salons, barbers, pet care, fitness, auto repair, detailing, and cleaning: none found.

### Inferences
- The "online first" share is likely highest for emergency and unfamiliar-provider purchases (HVAC breakdowns, plumbing leaks, storm-related roofing or tree work) and lowest for recurring, relationship-based services (barbers, pet care, lawn care with an existing crew).

### Gaps
- Mooresville-specific survey data: none found.
- Trade-level search-share data for most requested niches: none found.

## Source date and reliability summary
- BrightLocal 2026 (Feb 2026, n=1,002 US adults): most recent and most relevant. Figures are US-wide, not local.
- Silverback Strategies (Aug 2020, n=1,066): trade-relevant but older.
- Scorpion, ProRemodeler, Farnsworth, Software Advice, Modernize: undated or vendor-published; use directionally.
- Pew (Dec 2011), Kelsey/BizRate (2004), Kelsey/ComStat (2004), BIA/Kelsey Consumer Commerce Monitor (2013): old; use only for trend context.
- Google local conversion stats (76%, 28%, 78%, 88%): conflicting and attributed inconsistently; do not cite as fact.
