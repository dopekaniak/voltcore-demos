# Willingness to Pay for a Professional Website by Niche (Mooresville / Lake Norman, NC)

Research date: 2026-10-23. Scope: Mooresville, Huntersville, Cornelius, Davidson, Statesville, Concord. Almost all ticket and pricing figures below are national or vendor benchmarks, not Lake Norman data. Every figure labeled "estimate" is my inference, not a sourced number. Search tooling note: the Firecrawl search tool returned 401 errors, so all searches used the built-in WebSearch tool, which returns snippets only. Most pages were not fetched in full, so treat the numbers as directional.

## Key Question 1: Typical average ticket size by niche

### Takeaway
Ticket sizes vary by roughly two orders of magnitude, from about $100 to $300 for a lawn visit or drain clear to about $9,500 for a roof replacement. Sourced local (Lake Norman) ticket data was not found for any niche.

### Cited Findings
- HVAC residential service ticket: about $75 to $200 typical; repairs $150 to $2,500+ (secondary roundup, 2026). [Simpro roundup](https://simprogroup.com/blog/hvac-industry-statistics-2026)
- HVAC service-call fees: $70 to $200 in 2026, higher for nights, weekends, and emergencies. [Housecall Pro](https://www.housecallpro.com/?p=8017)
- HVAC average repair revenue per job rose from $818 (2021) to $1,205 (2025), about 24% higher after inflation (Housecall Pro, undated page, cites 2025 data). [Housecall Pro HVAC trends](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/hvac-industry-trends/)
- HVAC emergency calls about $385 vs. scheduled maintenance about $210, attributed to ServiceTitan platform data via a secondary site. Verify against ServiceTitan directly. [SchedulingKit](https://schedulingkit.com/statistics/hvac-industry-statistics)
- Light commercial service tickets estimated at 1.5 to 2.25x the residential average (about $475 to $800), per an expert answer, undated. [MyContractorUniversity](https://mycontractoruniversity.com/?p=127904)
- Roof replacement, full: average about $9,530, normal range $5,870 to $13,223 (Angi 2026 page). Other sources range from about $5,800 to $46,000. [Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/real-cost-new-roof.htm); [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/home-ownership/home-improvement/learn/roof-replacement-cost)
- Plumbing, drain clearing: Angi shows $95 to $500 (undated table); a separate Angi page puts the average near $200 (range $110 to $800). Angi's cost estimator page was last updated February 2022, so it is stale. [Angi plumbing](https://match.angi.com/cost/plumbing/); [Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-will-plumbing-repair-cost.htm)
- Plumbing, water heater: Angi repair range $150 to $750 (undated); a 2026 guide with weaker sourcing gives $900 to $3,000 for replacement. Plumber hourly rate about $100 plus trip fee; emergency rates about $150/hr plus a $100 to $350 trip fee. [Angi](https://www.angi.com/articles/how-much-will-plumbing-repair-cost.htm)
- Lawn care, monthly: $100 to $410 per month for maintenance (LawnStarter 2026 guide). Full packages with fertilization and weed control: $200 to $450 per month. Mowing-only visits: about $43 to $72 each. [LawnStarter](https://lawnstarter.com/blog/cost/lawn-care-price); [serviceagent guide, undated](https://serviceagent.ai/blogs/lawn-service-cost/)
- Pressure washing, residential: typically $150 to $500 (Housecall Pro guide, 2026). Driveways $100 to $300; house wash $250 to $500 single story, $400 to $800 two story. [Housecall Pro](https://housecallpro.com/resources/how-to-price-pressure-washing-jobs)
- Pest control, residential quarterly plan: $50 to $100 per visit, or about $200 to $600 per year (vendor blog). [CubeCreative](https://cubecreative.design/blog/pest-control-marketing/customer-retention-math)
- Barbershop, UK: £28 average per visit (aggregator, July 2026, survey not named). US barber cuts $15 to $40 per consumer article, with no survey source. [ZipDo](https://zipdo.co/uk-barber-industry-statistics/)
- Salon, per visit: $161.63 average (Mya survey, undated; method is unconventional). [American Salon / Mya coverage](https://www.americansalon.com/business/survey-reveals-missed-opportunities-salon-retail-sales)

### Inferences
- Estimate: Roofing (about $9,500) and HVAC replacement or repair (about $1,200 average repair) are the highest-ticket niches. Plumbing sits in the middle, with drain work low and water heater replacement high. Lawn care is a recurring $100 to $450 per month. Pressure washing and salon or barber visits are the lowest per transaction.
- Estimate: Lake Norman homes are larger and newer on average than the national stock, which may push ticket sizes somewhat above these national figures. No source confirmed this.

### Gaps
- No sourced ticket data found for electrical, painting, tree service, pool service, pet care, cleaning, handyman, auto repair, mobile detailing, restaurants, or fitness. These need separate research before ranking.
- No Lake Norman or Iredell-specific pricing found for any niche.
- Census County Business Patterns (CBP) for Iredell (FIPS 37097) was not retrieved. The 2022 CBP was released June 27, 2024 ([Census CBP 2022](https://www.census.gov/data/datasets/2022/econ/cbp/2022-cbp.html)), but the search returned no Iredell figures. Check data.census.gov, filtered to FIPS 37097 and NAICS sectors 238 (specialty trade), 561730 (landscaping), 811 (repair), 812 (personal services), 722 (food services).

## Key Question 2: What local businesses pay for websites, SEO, or marketing

### Takeaway
Published pricing is sparse and mostly vendor-sourced. Local agencies appear to list project minimums under $1,000, and national "basic" packages sit at $500 to $3,000, which overlaps with the target range. Home-service marketing budgets are benchmarked at 8% to 15% of revenue, but these figures come from vendor blogs.

### Cited Findings
- Mooresville-area agencies on UpCity list project minimums under $1,000 (Wave Design Firm, Mooresville-based, monthly retainer under $500; Carolina Web Pro, Carolinas-based, 6 reviews). These are sponsored profiles, so the figures are provider-reported. [Wave Design Firm listing](https://agencyapp.dmbx.aws.gartner.com/profiles/wave-design-firm); [Carolina Web Pro listing](https://agencyapp.dmbx.aws.gartner.com/local-marketing-agencies/profiles/carolina-web-pro)
- Basic website packages, $500 to $3,000, typically up to five pages with responsive design, basic SEO, and a contact form. Source is a general web-design pricing page, undated. [ABSTRAKT MG](https://www.abstraktmg.com/?p=56636)
- A Charlotte-area designer quotes $1,500 to $10,000 for a professional custom site, depending on scope and platform. Ecommerce can exceed $10,000. [Adam SWP](https://adamswp.com/what-does-a-website-actually-cost-in-charlotte/)
- Home-service marketing budgets: 8% to 12% (Gartner and Deloitte/Duke, cited in a sector chart); 8% to 15% (HVAC, plumbing, roofing); 9% to 12% for B2C service companies (HubSpot view); 10% to 20% (WebFX). These are vendor-cited and not verified. [CubeCreative](https://cubecreative.design/blog/small-business-marketing/marketing-budget-home-service-business); [WebFX handbook](https://www.webfx.com/blog/home-services/marketing-budget-handbook/)
- New small businesses: 26.1% of 1,938 signups reported zero monthly marketing spend (UENI, 2026). General small business, not home services. [UENI](https://ueni.com/blog/small-business-marketing-budget/)
- Pest control acquisition cost: about $250 per customer on average; termite about $500; general pest about $200 (vendor source). [CubeCreative](https://cubecreative.design/blog/pest-control-marketing/customer-retention-math)

### Inferences
- Estimate: A $500 to $3,000 site is within the local market range, but the bottom of that range is where cheap competitors price. Businesses that spend 8% to 15% of revenue on marketing and have a $1,200 HVAC repair ticket can likely afford a one-time site cost. A barbershop or pressure washer on a $200 ticket has less room.
- Estimate: Recurring-revenue niches (pest control, lawn care) are more likely to fund a website as an acquisition cost, because each customer's lifetime value justifies a few hundred dollars of acquisition spend.

### Gaps
- No Mooresville-specific price list found. The UpCity figures are the only local data point.
- No primary survey of local home-service website spend was found.

## Key Question 3: Recurring, high-value customers that raise a website's value

### Takeaway
Pest control and lawn care have the clearest recurring models with sourced pricing. HVAC and plumbing have maintenance agreements, but I found no sourced data on their size. Pest control LTV estimates are wide and depend heavily on retention figures, which conflict across sources.

### Cited Findings
- Pest control: 85.4% of residential service revenue came from recurring service in 2025 (NPMA, cited via a vendor blog). A separate NPMA/PCO study puts recurring revenue at 74% of total income across surveyed firms. The gap reflects scope (residential only vs. all income). [CubeCreative](https://cubecreative.design/blog/pest-control-marketing/customer-retention-math)
- Pest control LTV: $1,500 to $3,000 for a healthy mid-size residential customer in one source; $600 to $1,200 in another; $2,847 in a software vendor's illustrative blueprint. [FieldProxy](https://www.fieldproxy.ai/blueprints/pest-control-customer-value)
- Pest control retention conflicts: 82% to 87% target (one source); about 40% annual churn (another); 60% to 80% retention (FinModelsLab, cited by a vendor). [CubeCreative](https://cubecreative.design/blog/pest-control-marketing/customer-retention-math)
- Lawn care: recurring weekly or biweekly plans at $70 to $220 per month for mowing; full programs $200 to $450 per month. [LawnStarter](https://lawnstarter.com/blog/cost/lawn-care-price)
- Roofing and HVAC replacement are one-time or storm-driven, with lower recurrence. HVAC maintenance visits run about $210 each per the ServiceTitan figure above (secondary source).

### Inferences
- Estimate: Pest control has the highest LTV per customer among these niches on recurring revenue, roughly $600 to $3,000 (wide, retention-driven). A website that brings in even a few customers a year would likely pay back a $500 to $3,000 build.
- Estimate: A roofing lead has a high single-job value (about $9,500) but low repeat value, so the website justifies itself on one job.

### Gaps
- No sourced HVAC or plumbing maintenance-agreement pricing or retention figures.
- Retention data for pest control is contradictory and vendor-sourced; treat LTV as a range, not a point estimate.
