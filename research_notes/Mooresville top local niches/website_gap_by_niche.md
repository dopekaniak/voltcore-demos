# Website Gap by Niche: Mooresville / Lake Norman (Iredell and Mecklenburg Counties)

Research date: 2026-10-23. Scope: which local niches are most likely to lack a real standalone website or to rely only on Facebook, Instagram, Yelp, Nextdoor, or Google Business Profile.

**Bottom line for the report writer:** No public source gives a count or share of Mooresville-area businesses without a website, by niche. The only local-level evidence is a handful of directory and search-snippet checks (small samples, not random). Everything below labeled "Inference" is a judgment built on those samples and on national surveys, not a measured local rate. The most useful next step is a manual or licensed count (see Gaps).

**Method limits:**
- Google Maps counts were not pulled. Scraping Google Maps violates its terms, and the Firecrawl search tool returned HTTP 401 (not authenticated) during this session.
- Census County Business Patterns (CBP) API returned a "missing key" redirect (HTTP 302 to `missing_key.html`) without a Census API key. Industry-level county counts were therefore not retrieved.
- Directory pages were read through WebFetch, which captured the page as rendered at fetch time. Listing dates are unknown.
- The Yellow Pages, Facebook, and web-search results were read from search snippets, not opened page by page.

## Q1. Which niches have many listings with no website link, or only a social profile?

### Takeaway
Lawn care, landscaping, and pressure washing show the clearest local evidence of social-first operation. HVAC, plumbing, and electrical have more established firms with real websites, but the directory data for them is weak evidence because the directory formats do not show website fields. Treat the niche ranking below as a hypothesis to verify, not a measured result.

## Q2. Are there published statistics that apply to these niches?

### Takeaway
Only general small-business website statistics exist with a credible source. The best available is Clutch (2025): 17% of US small businesses lack a website. No credible published rate by trade was found. The only trade-level figures available are from a vendor scan of businesses that already have sites, which cannot measure the no-website rate.

### Cited Findings
- Clutch (fielded 2025-08-05, n=406, US): 17% of small businesses lack a website; the top reason was "Not relevant to my industry" (34%). No industry breakdown is published in the article. [Clutch](https://clutch.co/resources/state-of-small-business-websites-2025)
- NFIB (March 2025, via search summary; not opened): 82% of US small businesses had a website. [NFIB, via search summary]
- Superior Marketing home-services scan (August 2026, 1,000 contractor sites, vendor): by trade, no analytics: roofing 77%, HVAC 78%, plumbing 71%, electrical 58% of sites scanned. These are sites that already exist. [Contracting Business summary](https://www.contractingbusiness.com/industry-news/news/55401085/new-study-finds-hvac-marketing-tracking-gaps)
- Census CBP 2023 (released 2025-06-26): Iredell County QuickFacts shows 5,405 total employer establishments and 17,597 nonemployer establishments for 2023 (county-level, all industries). Figures taken from the search summary of the QuickFacts page; not opened. [Census QuickFacts Iredell](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/table/PST045216/37097); [Census CBP release](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2025/2023-county-business-patterns.html)

### Inferences
- Clutch's 17% is the most defensible starting baseline for a generic small business. Because the sample is national and not by trade, it cannot be applied to a niche without an adjustment. A reasonable use is to treat it as a floor for owner-operator trades and test it locally.
- The Clutch "not relevant to my industry" answer (34%) suggests the no-website share may be higher in trades where owners see a site as unnecessary, but the survey does not link this answer to any trade.
- Vendor scans cannot give a no-website share because they only sample sites that exist. Do not cite them as evidence of how many businesses lack a site.

### Gaps
- No published rate of no-website businesses by trade for the US or NC was found.
- The NC OSBM CBP dataset page (linc.osbm.nc.gov) was fetched but did not show NAICS-level Iredell counts in the rendered text. The data explorer or API would be needed.
- The Census API returned a missing-key redirect. A free Census API key is required for NAICS-level county pulls (not obtained or used here).

## Q3. Which niches have the most listings in the area (more listings = more leads)?

### Takeaway
No listing counts were verified for any niche in Mooresville, Huntersville, Cornelius, Davidson, Statesville, or Concord. The only verified local count is the chamber HVAC category (14 results). The county-level CBP figure is for all industries, and the Census API needed a key.

### Cited Findings
- Mooresville Chamber HVAC category: 14 results. [Chamber](https://business.mooresvillenc.org/list/category/air-conditioning-heating-contractors-219)
- Yahoo Local HVAC, Mooresville: page 3 of 5+ shown; total not displayed. [Yahoo Local](https://local.yahoo.com/hvac-contractors/nc/mooresville/3/)
- Yellow Pages Mooresville lawn care and pressure cleaning pages: capped at "Best 30" display; total not displayed. [Yellow Pages](https://www.yellowpages.com/mooresville-nc/lawn-care-services)
- Iredell County: 5,405 employer establishments, all industries, 2023 (search summary of QuickFacts). [Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/table/PST045216/37097)

### Inferences
- Likely high-count niches to verify first: HVAC, plumbing, lawn care/landscaping, auto repair, restaurants, and hair/beauty. These are common owner-operated trades in suburban markets, but this is not a measured ranking.
- The county CBP figure cannot isolate the Lake Norman area. Mecklenburg County (which contains Huntersville, Cornelius, and most of Davidson) includes all of Charlotte, and Concord is in Cabarrus County (FIPS 37025), not Iredell or Mecklenburg. Any CBP pull needs three counties (Iredell 37097, Mecklenburg 37119, Cabarrus 37025) and still will not separate the towns.

### Gaps
- No Google Maps counts. Google Maps has no public counting API for this, and scraping it violates its terms. The safe approach is a manual search per town and niche, recording the number of results and whether each has a website link.
- No Yelp, BBB, Angi, Thumbtack, or Nextdoor counts were gathered. Yelp and Nextdoor limit automated access; manual counts would be needed.
- Census CBP NAICS pulls (planned codes: 238220 plumbing/HVAC, 238210 electrical, 561730 landscaping, 811111/811192 auto repair, 812112 hair salons, 812190 other personal care/pet care, 722 food services) need a Census API key.
