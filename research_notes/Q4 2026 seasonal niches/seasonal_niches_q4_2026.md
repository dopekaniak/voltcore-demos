# Q4 2026 seasonal niches for LaunchLocal (research date 2026-10-23)

Scope: fall and winter seasonal businesses in the Mooresville / Lake Norman area that could use a site or booking page.
Evidence: mostly 2025 or older, vendor and marketing sources. No 2026 local demand data was found.

## Windows and timing
| Niche | Demand window | Site needed by | Ticket | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Christmas light installers | Sept to Dec; prime demand through mid-Nov | Now | Low ($208-500 per install, Thumbtack Oct 2024-Oct 2025 data) | 64% of homeowners plan to hang lights (This Old House, Nov 2025); 72% of those DIY |
| Christmas tree lots | Open around Thanksgiving to Dec 24 | ~Nov 20 | Medium | Artificial trees: 83% of homes used one in 2025 (ACTA). Western NC farms recovering from Helene. |
| Holiday pie and bakery pre-orders | Pre-orders close late Oct to mid-Nov; pickup last week of Nov | Early Nov | Medium-high | Cutoffs cluster Nov 16-17 (Chronogram). Many bakers take pre-orders on social media. |
| Gutter cleaning / leaf removal | Oct into early winter | Now | Low-medium (~$150 per visit, one source, possibly a single origin) | Recurring; upsells to winter prep |
| Pumpkin sellers / carvers | Sept to Oct 31 | Too late | Low | Season ends in about 8 days |

## Product implication
Most tickets are below the $500-3,000 cost of a standard site. A low-cost one-page booking or pre-order page, live in days and sold in batches, may fit better. Unvalidated.
