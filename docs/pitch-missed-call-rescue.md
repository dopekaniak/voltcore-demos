# Pitch: Missed-Call Rescue (monthly)

**Who it's for:** owner-run trades (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, garage doors) that miss calls while on a job.
**Price:** $297/month, no contract. Setup fee: [decide before first call].

## Opener
"Hi [Owner], this is [Name]. Quick question: when you're on a job and the phone rings, what happens to that call?"

## The problem (say it once, plainly)
When nobody picks up, the caller usually tries the next company on Google. You don't know how many calls that is, and we can measure it together during the pilot.

## What it does
- A missed call gets a text back within seconds.
- The text asks what they need and whether it's urgent.
- It checks the ZIP against your service area.
- It books a time slot, or flags urgent calls to you by text right away.
- Gas smell, CO, or fire goes straight to you, with a 911 safety reply to the customer.

## The ask
"Give me 10 minutes and I'll text your own number from the demo, so you see exactly what your customers get."

## Objections
- **"We answer every call."** "Nice. What about evenings and weekends? That's when no-heat calls come in."
- **"Jobber/Housecall Pro already does this."** "If you already use it, keep it. Most owners pay for it and never set it up. I handle setup and keep the replies tuned so they actually book."
- **"Is this a bot?"** "It's a text reply that sounds like your office. You approve the wording, and any urgent call goes to you."
- **"How much?"** "$297 a month, no contract. If it doesn't book you any work in 30 days, you can cancel."

## Before you say the 30-day guarantee
Only offer it if you can actually honor it. Decide the terms first.

## Don't say
- Any missed-call percentage or revenue figure unless you have data from the client's own calls.
- "Verified" or "guaranteed" anything you haven't checked.
- That texts can go out before A2P registration is approved.

## Next step
Book a 15-minute call or a 10-minute demo on the owner's own phone.
