# Pitch: Estimate Revival (performance-based)

**Who it's for:** contractors who have old estimates that never closed (usually HVAC, roofing, remodeling, with 20+ quotes sitting unanswered).
**Price:** 10% of net invoiced amount on jobs we attribute to revival. No setup fee, no monthly fee.

## Opener
"Hi [Owner], this is [Name]. How many estimates did you send last year that never turned into a job?"

## The problem (let them answer first)
Most shops don't have time to call old quotes back. The customer already asked for a price, so these are warm leads that went cold.

## What it does
- You export your estimate history from your system (Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, or QuickBooks).
- We rank the open estimates and write a call script for each one.
- Your staff makes the calls, using the script we approved together.
- Anyone who says no or asks to stop is removed from the list permanently.
- When a revived estimate closes, the fee applies to that job, and you get a monthly statement.

## The ask
"Can we take 20 of your old estimates and run a two-week pilot? You'll see the list and the scripts before anything goes out."

## Objections
- **"We already follow up."** "How far back do you go? Most shops stop after one call. The money is in the 3-to-12-month-old quotes nobody touches."
- **"I don't want someone calling my customers."** "Totally fair. Your staff makes the calls, you approve the script, and anyone who says no is off the list for good."
- **"I can't export my data."** "Most systems export in a couple of clicks. Tell me which one you use and I'll walk you through it."
- **"What counts as a job from you?"** "A job is attributed only if the customer was on the list, was contacted through the program, and booked within 60 days. That's written into the agreement, so there's no guessing."

## Before you pitch
- Read the telecom review checklist first (`docs/telecom-lawyer-brief.md`). Don't run any calls until counsel has answered the questions in it.
- Have the draft contract ready (`docs/contractor-agreement-draft.md`), and fill in the blanks with counsel.

## Don't say
- "Guaranteed" bookings or revenue.
- Any close rate or recovery percentage as a promise. These are industry ranges, not results.
- That we've already worked with other contractors unless you have a reference who agrees to it.
