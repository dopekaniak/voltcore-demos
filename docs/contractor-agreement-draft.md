## 1. Parties and scope

1.1 **Provider:** [Founder legal name / entity], "Provider."
1.2 **Contractor:** [Business legal name], "Contractor."
1.3 **Services:** Provider will help Contractor re-contact past customers who received a written estimate from Contractor and did not book work ("Revival Services"), and/or handle missed-call text replies from prospective customers ("Rescue Services"), each as selected in Schedule A.
1.4 **Contractor's staff** makes all outbound calls in the Revival Services unless Schedule A states otherwise. Provider does not place calls to end customers.

---

## 2. Scripts and approval

2.1 Provider supplies call scripts, voicemail text, and any text messages. **No script is used until Contractor approves it in writing** (email is sufficient). Approval is recorded in Schedule B with a date.
2.2 Contractor may not change an approved script's factual claims (pricing, warranties, timelines) without written approval from Provider.
2.3 Contractor's staff will not use an automated or auto-dialing system to place Revival calls, and will not use any prerecorded or AI-generated audio unless Schedule B expressly approves it after counsel review.
2.4 Each party will promptly stop any use of a script that either reasonably believes is unlawful or misleading.

---

## 3. Customer list and data

3.1 **Source.** Contractor will export its customer and estimate records from its own system (for example Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, or QuickBooks). Provider will not purchase, scrape, or import lists from any third party.
3.2 **Warranty.** Contractor warrants that: (a) it created or lawfully received each record it provides; (b) each record reflects a real estimate Contractor gave to that person; (c) Contractor has the right to use the records for the purposes in this Agreement; and (d) Contractor has not provided records of anyone who has asked not to be contacted by Contractor.
3.3 **Consent records.** Contractor will identify, for each customer record, whether Contractor holds written consent to text that customer, and the date and source of that consent. Provider will send texts only to records marked with consent.
3.4 **Ownership.** As between the parties, Contractor owns its customer data. Provider owns its scripts, copilot tooling, call-outcome logs, and the performance-fee ledger, but Provider may use anonymized, aggregated outcome data to improve its services. Provider will not sell customer data.
3.5 **Retention.** Each party keeps customer data only as long as needed for this Agreement and as required by law. [Counsel to set a retention period for call logs.] Upon termination, Provider deletes or returns Contractor's data within [30] days, except records it must keep for legal or accounting reasons.
3.6 **Security.** Each party will protect customer data with reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards.

---

## 4. Opt-outs and suppression

4.1 Any request to stop contact (including "STOP," "don't call me," or similar words) is honored **permanently** across all of Contractor's campaigns and Provider's services.
4.2 Contractor will forward any opt-out it receives directly to Provider within [1] business day. Provider adds it to a suppression list that is checked before every call or text.
4.3 Contractor will not re-contact anyone on the suppression list through any channel.

---

## 5. Calling and texting rules

5.1 All calls and texts will be placed only between **9:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.** in the customer's local time, and never on Sunday.
5.2 Contractor will not call numbers on the National Do-Not-Call Registry or any internal do-not-call list unless counsel has confirmed in writing that an exception applies to that record.
5.3 Provider will not send any text until the texting number is registered under A2P 10DLC rules and the registration is approved.
5.4 Each party will comply with the TCPA, the FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule, and state telemarketing and consumer-protection laws that apply to the calls and texts in scope.
5.5 [Counsel: insert state-specific requirements for the states in Schedule A.]

## 6. Performance fee (Revival Services)

6.1 **Definition of Attributed Job.** A job is "attributed" to Provider only if all of the following are true:
   (a) the customer was on Contractor's estimate list and was contacted through the Revival Services;
   (b) the customer booked a visit or signed a work order within **[60] days** after the first Revival contact, as recorded in Contractor's system; and
   (c) the customer did not already book or decline the same work before that first contact.
6.2 **Fee.** Contractor will pay Provider **[10]%** of the net invoiced amount of each Attributed Job, excluding taxes, permits, financing fees, refunds, and warranty work.
6.3 **Statement and payment.** Provider will send a monthly statement listing each Attributed Job (lead ID, amount, date). Contractor has [15] days to dispute an item in writing. Undisputed amounts are due within [30] days after the statement.
6.4 **Disputes.** Attribution disputes go to a named contact at each party. If unresolved in [10] business days, they go to [mediation / arbitration per Section 10].
6.5 **Audit.** Provider may review the job records behind any statement, on [10] business days' notice, no more than once per quarter.
6.6 **Cap.** [Optional: fees per month capped at $[amount].]

## 7. Rescue Services (if selected)

7.1 Provider's monthly fee is **$[297]**, billed in advance, plus any setup fee in Schedule A.
7.2 Provider will respond to inbound text replies within [the times in Schedule A].
7.3 Emergency texts (including gas smell, carbon monoxide, fire, or flooding) trigger an immediate alert to Contractor's designated contact and an automated safety reply directing the customer to 911 or the gas utility. Contractor is responsible for responding to emergency alerts.

## 8. Provider's representations and limits

8.1 Provider will perform the Services in a professional manner consistent with this Agreement and applicable law.
8.2 **No guarantee of results.** Provider does not guarantee any number of booked jobs, revenue, or conversion rate.
8.3 Provider is not responsible for Contractor's pricing, workmanship, or customer relationships.

---

## 9. Liability, indemnity, and insurance

9.1 **Contractor indemnity.** Contractor will defend and indemnify Provider against claims arising from (a) the accuracy of customer data under Section 3.2; (b) Contractor's or its staff's calls or texts that deviate from approved scripts; and (c) Contractor's failure to honor opt-outs it received.
9.2 **Provider indemnity.** Provider will defend and indemnify Contractor against claims arising from Provider's scripts as approved by Contractor, to the extent the claim is caused by a legal violation in the approved script itself.
9.3 **Limitation.** Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages. Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid under this Agreement in the prior [6] months, except for indemnity obligations and violations of Sections 3 or 4.
9.4 **Insurance.** Provider will maintain [E&O / cyber / TCPA coverage in the amount of $__] before the first Revival call. Contractor will maintain its general liability insurance.
